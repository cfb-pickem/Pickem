League Rules

Format

  • Every week: 4 games against the spread
  • Picks lock: at the scheduled start of the week’s first game — a weather or TV delay doesn’t buy you more time. Everyone’s picks stay hidden until then, and are revealed at that moment. In the playoffs each game locks at its own scheduled start.
  • Lines lock: Tuesday 11:00 AM CT. Whatever the line says then is what the week is scored against. A game with no line yet keeps the first one posted.
  • Playoffs: Top 4 advance, scores reset, pick all playoff games

Payouts

  • 1st: Remainder of buy-in
  • 2nd: Buy-in + $25
  • 3rd: Buy-in back

Tiebreakers

Regular Season

  1. Conference championship game wins
  2. Army v Navy
  3. Celebration Bowl
  4. Win % last 8 games
  5. Coin flip

Playoffs

  1. Army-Navy (if unused)
  2. Regular season points
  3. Other bowl games
