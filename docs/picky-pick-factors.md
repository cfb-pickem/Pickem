# Picky pick factors (ATS)

Charlie, 2026-11-16: "Brainstorm like 50 other factors that go into football games including Ross news. Those all need to be considered in his picks."

The league is graded only against the spread, so each factor counts for how it moves the margin vs the line, not who wins. Each one needs a real data source, computed in code. A factor with no source gets skipped, never guessed.

## Line and market
1. League locked line (Tue noon) vs current market line: the value gap
2. Opening line vs current line, size and direction of the move
3. Cause of the move (injury, weather, news) when the news shows one
4. Key numbers (3, 7, 10, 14): does the league line sit on the good side of one the market crossed
5. Total (O/U) and implied team totals: big spread + low total = harder cover
6. Late or steam moves near kickoff (after the league locks)
7. Public vs sharp split (only if a public source exists)

## Personnel
8. Starting QB status (injury, questionable, benched)
9. Backup QB experience if the starter is out
10. Other key injuries: RB1, WR1, OL starters, edge rusher, CB1
11. Suspensions and academic ineligibility
12. Late-season opt-outs and draft prospects sitting
13. Transfer-portal departures midseason
14. Depth-chart changes in the last two weeks

## Coaching and situation
15. Coaching change, interim coach, or a coach already announced as leaving
16. Hot-seat coach (Dellenger's carousel reporting): playing for the job vs checked out
17. Coach's ATS record overall, as a favorite, and as an underdog
18. Off a bye vs opponent on normal rest
19. Short week (Thursday/Friday games)
20. Lookahead spot: big game next week
21. Letdown spot: after a huge win or a rivalry game
22. Sandwich game between two big games
23. Revenge game (lost to this opponent last year, especially if embarrassed)
24. Rivalry game (underdogs historically cover more)
25. Bowl eligibility on the line (5-win teams)
26. CFP / conference-title stakes; clinched or eliminated teams
27. Senior Day / last home game
28. Committee "style points" incentive to run up the score (helps big favorites cover)

## Travel and environment
29. Home-field strength (stadium, crowd, home ATS record)
30. Travel distance and time zones (a West Coast team at an 11am CT kickoff)
31. Kickoff time; night game
32. Altitude (Air Force, Wyoming, Colorado, Utah, Boise)
33. Wind (hurts passing and totals)
34. Rain, snow, or cold for a warm-weather or dome team
35. Turf vs grass
36. Neutral site

## Matchup stats (CFBD advanced)
37. Offensive EPA/play vs the opponent's defensive EPA/play
38. Success rate on both sides
39. Explosiveness allowed and created
40. Rushing offense vs run defense (line yards, stuff rate)
41. Rushing QB vs the defense's QB-run yards allowed
42. Passing offense vs pass defense
43. Havoc rate and sacks (OL vs DL)
44. Third-down conversion rates
45. Red-zone touchdown rate (scores 7s or settles for 3s: key for covers)
46. Special teams and kicker reliability
47. Turnover margin and luck (fumble-recovery rate regresses)
48. Tempo: plays per game (more plays = more variance in big spreads)
49. Opponent-adjusted ratings (SP+, FPI, or CFBD SRS) and the line those ratings imply
50. Recent form (last 3-4) vs full season: regression signals
51. Head-to-head history, straight up and ATS
52. Style clash: option or service-academy offense vs a defense that hasn't seen it
53. Backdoor and garbage-time risk: does the favorite pull starters; does the dog score late

## Off-field and Dellenger news
54. Realignment bitterness (a team leaving its conference, a grudge game)
55. Revenue-sharing / NIL roster turmoil and midseason departures
56. Program turmoil: investigations, AD changes, coach-search distraction
57. CFP format and seeding incentives (bye race, resting starters late)

## League game theory
58. Key rivals' past tendencies (favorites/dogs, home teams, their own fan team, following the crowd)
59. League consensus on the game (from past patterns): fade it or follow it
60. The asker's standings need: chasing = take variance and contrarian picks; leading = match the field
